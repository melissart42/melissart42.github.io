We are passionate about play

Mm isn't all (just) pink carpets and ice cream - we take our work seriously!

Our values are fundamental to what makes Mm an award-winning studio, empowering our people while continuously striving for excellence.

Inclusivity

Central to our studio and product design. We foster a safe and positive space at work and within our communities, enabling people to be their authentic selves.

Collaboration

We delight in open collaboration and drive innovation with creative jamming, in the studio and with our community.

Aspiration

We are positively ambitious and take creative risks, allowing us to build games which delight our audiences and bring success to our people.

Creativity

We seek originality by empowering creative exploration at all levels and across disciplines to generate new creative sparks and contribute to the overall success of the studio.

Respect

In our game design, people's time, work, feedback, culture, wellbeing, the planet... We show respect for our peers and players, maintaining a positive and ethical studio culture.
